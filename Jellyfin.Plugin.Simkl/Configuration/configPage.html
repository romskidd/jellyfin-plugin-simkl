Scrobbling
Reports start, pause and stop to Simkl as you watch. The title shows in your "Watching now" banner and is marked watched automatically when you stop past 80%.
Rewatches
When you finish something you had already watched, it is filed as a rewatch on Simkl instead of doing nothing. Simkl Pro or VIP only. Your Simkl account is on the free plan, so this option is not available.
Import from Simkl (experimental)
Episodes and movies watched on Simkl are marked played in Jellyfin, matched by IMDb/TMDb/TVDB ids (anime is left out for now). Tick, Save, then Preview to see what would change, then Apply initial import. Later passes run after a playback or a library scan, at most once an hour, and stop at 200 changes until you confirm. Every pass can be undone for 7 days.
Only touches items this import marked itself, and only on a full pass (Preview then Apply), never on the hourly delta. Off by default.
