How it works

Each step stands on its own: run only step 1 to bring your Simkl history into Jellyfin, only step 2 to send Jellyfin's history to Simkl, or both. Step 3 is optional and only for those who want both sides kept aligned from then on.

  1. Simkl to Jellyfin. Everything you watched on Simkl gets marked played here, with the original dates. Items you don't have yet are remembered and marked when they arrive after a library scan.
  2. Jellyfin to Simkl. Everything already marked played here is sent to your Simkl history with its dates. Items that came from step 1 are skipped.
  3. Keep in sync. Once both are done, tick the box. From then on, what you watch on Simkl shows up here (checked after a playback or a library scan, at most once an hour), and what you watch here already reaches Simkl in real time.

Every step shows a preview first and can be undone for 7 days. Matching uses IMDb/TMDb/TVDB ids only; anime is left out for now. Automatic passes stop at 200 changes until you confirm.
